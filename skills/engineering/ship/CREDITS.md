# Credits

`ship` is Keelwork's own skill, but two of its parts are adapted from MIT-licensed sources. Their notices are reproduced below.

- **Discovery and red-team** ([DISCOVERY.md](DISCOVERY.md)) condenses the `brainstorm-ideas-new` and `brainstorm-ideas-existing` skills (pm-product-discovery) and the `strategy-red-team` skill (pm-execution) from [phuryn/pm-skills](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills), by Pawel Huryn. The full skills ship in Keelwork's productivity bucket under their own names.
- **The run loop and ledger** ([RUN-LOOP.md](RUN-LOOP.md), [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md)) draw on the `subagent-driven-development` skill in [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), by Jesse Vincent: a fresh implementer per task, a spec review before the quality review, a capped fix loop, decisions recorded as rulings, and a ledger that survives compaction. Keelwork's version adds parking instead of stopping, an autonomy contract, and a morning report.
